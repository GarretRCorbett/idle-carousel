# Theme draft: the Ticket (Garret's pick, 2026-09-26)

**Status:** exploring. Garret likes the ticket mascot most; the **light bulb** spirit (a rim bulb
flickering back to life) is the backup. Nothing here is decided until Garret says so; Codex will be
asked for thoughts (framing, mascots in cozy incrementals, Wildfrost-style guides) once its usage is
back. Game-facing names and text are Garret's to approve.

## Why a ticket
- Already in the game: **ticket booths** pay the Gold, and the **Lucky Ticket** is an event.
- A mascot that's a **simple shape** (Garret: like Wildfrost's small guide characters), so it's drawn in
  code now, never locked to an art pack, and any theme can re-skin it with a color and a stamp.

## The mascot
- A paper admission ticket: perforated edges, "ADMIT ONE", a torn stub.
- Eyes made from **conductor's punch holes**; a punch-hole mouth; it can tilt, hop and flap its stub.
- Personality: an eager usher who loves the ride, cheers you on, and gets flustered when debris
  grabs the rim.
- Name ideas: **Stub**, **Tix**, **Punch**, **Admit**, **Tilly**. (Garret: a name would be nice, not required.)

## How the theme could grow from it (ideas, not scope)
- **Premise:** the old park is reopening; you're bringing its carousel back, and the ticket is your
  guest pass for the season. (Fits the GDD's restoration arc: debris and rust, the Rusted King,
  "Restoration Complete".)
- **Stamps as progress:** each boss beaten (later, achievements) stamps or punches your ticket; you see
  it fill up.
- **The Park Guide → a "Ticket Book":** discoveries as stubs in your book. Also answers Garret's note
  that "park" is used everywhere.
- **Prestige → a new season:** a fresh ticket (new color) that keeps your old stubs.
- **Events:** the Lucky Ticket as the mascot's cousin.
- **Seasonal themes:** a winter ticket (icy blue, snowflake stamp), a Halloween ticket (orange, pumpkin
  stamp): cheap to make, and they fit `planning/phase3/theming_plan.md`.
- **Tutorial:** the ticket is the keeper; lines get a final pass in its voice.
