# Backstory (reference for devlogs; not a post)

Garret's notes, 2026-09-25. Use pieces of this in posts where they fit. **Anonymity:** the GDD plans an anonymous release, so don't name the earlier projects or link them in public posts unless Garret decides to go public; they tie back to his real name.

- **The spark:** his daughters love carousels. Posts mention them lightly, no names.
- **Carousel Crusade** (the first attempt): a carousel game in the spirit of Slay the Spire and Wildfrost, started when his oldest was 3. Built in Godot, and Garret learned a lot, but the scope was far too big. He made a Steam page for it then ("Carousel Crusade", app 4094570, developer "Corbett's Castle").
  - **Its placeholder capsule art was AI-generated.** It must never be used or shown anywhere. If that Steam app is ever reused, replace every graphical asset.
- **Dunk or Crumble** (Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2, theme "Risk it for the biscuit"): a spinning plate of biscuits you bet on and dunk. This is where he fell in love with the spin mechanic. https://garretrcorbett.itch.io/dunk-or-crumble
- **The Juniper Dev jam** (theme "spin to win"), a few months ago: he made another spinning game. It wasn't submitted (not finished in time), but he loved making it and decided to try again for Steam.
- **Idle Carousel:** an idle game felt finishable. He planned it for a couple of months (the GDD), then development started.
- **Dream:** if Idle Carousel reaches Steam and even 3 to 5 people buy it and like it, back to Carousel Crusade.
- **Brand idea:** a year-ago search found very few carousel games. The dream is a carousel game studio making carousel games in many genres. Studio name idea: "Corbett's Castle" (it contains his surname, so keep it out of public posts while anonymous).
- **Voice for posts:** casual and warm, the way Garret writes messages. Exclamation points, "haha", ":p", "lol", "honestly", "waaaay", and small asides and winks, like the park-map nod "(can you recognize where? ;))".
